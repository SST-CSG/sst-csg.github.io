Alfredo Pinelli is Professor of Fluid Simulation in the Department of Engineering and leads the Fluid Mechanics Group. He served as Associate Dean for Research of the School of Science & Technology from 2022 to 2025.

He studied aeronautical engineering at Politecnico di Milano, took the diploma course at the Von Karman Institute for Fluid Dynamics and received his PhD from the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne. Before joining City in 2013 he held academic posts at universities in Madrid and headed the Numerical Simulation Unit at CIEMAT.

His research uses numerical simulation to study turbulence, environmental flows and the interaction between fluids and structures.
