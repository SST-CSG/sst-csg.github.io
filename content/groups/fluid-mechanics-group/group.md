The group studies fluid flows through high-fidelity numerical simulation. Its work covers turbulence, flows over and through complex surfaces such as canopies and porous walls, and the interaction between fluids and flexible structures.

The group develops its own simulation methods, including immersed-boundary techniques, and runs them on high performance computers. Recent work also applies machine learning to the control and modelling of turbulent flows.
